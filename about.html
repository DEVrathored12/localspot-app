Our Story

About LocalSpot

We're on a mission to put local businesses on the map — literally. LocalSpot connects customers with the best shops in their neighbourhood through rich media, real reviews, and instant contact.

Who We Are

Built for Local Businesses

LocalSpot was founded in 2024 with a simple belief: every great local shop deserves to be discovered. We saw talented shop owners struggling to compete with big e-commerce platforms, and we built a solution that plays to their strengths — real products, real people, real locations.

Today we serve 1,200+ shops across 48 neighbourhoods in Mumbai, with plans to expand across India. Our platform connects shops with customers and local video creators, creating a thriving hyperlocal ecosystem.
