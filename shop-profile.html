About the Shop

The Spice Route is Mumbai's most trusted spice and dry fruit store, serving over 10,000 families since 1978. We source our spices directly from farms across India — cardamom from Kerala, saffron from Kashmir, black pepper from Wayanad.

Our range includes over 200 varieties of spices, masalas, dry fruits, seeds, and herbs. All products are hygienically packed and lab-tested for quality.
